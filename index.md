# yapping

A dictation keyboard for iPhone and iPad. Hold the bar, talk, and your words
land in whatever you're typing in, cleaned up in your own style.

yapping uses your own [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) API key.
Transcription and cleanup run on your OpenRouter account, billed there.

## Support

Report a problem or ask a question on
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/y0mingzhang/yapping/issues/new). Include the
app you were typing in, whether Cleanup was on, what you expected, and what
happened. Don't paste your API key.

## Setup

1. Open yapping, paste your OpenRouter key, and allow the microphone.
2. Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → yapping.
3. Tap yapping in that list and turn on **Allow Full Access**. The keyboard
   needs it to reach the app, which does the recording.
4. In any text field, switch to yapping and tap **Turn on mic**. yapping opens
   and turns the mic on; tap **Back** at the top left to return.
5. Hold the bar while you talk and let go to finish.

## Common problems

- **Turn on mic every time.** iOS only lets a running app record for a
  keyboard. The mic stays on until you turn it off, but iOS ends it on a call,
  for Siri, or when it needs memory. Tap Turn on mic again.
- **"Allow Full Access".** The letters work without it; dictation doesn't.
- **"API key rejected".** Paste a working key in yapping → Settings.
- **The mic indicator stays on.** That's the mic being ready between takes.
  Tap the power key on the keyboard, or Turn off in the app.
